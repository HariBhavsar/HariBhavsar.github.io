Teaching

I have been a teaching assistant for the following courses

IIT Bombay | CS231: Digital Logic Design and Computer Architecture | Instructor: Biswabandan Panda
Semester: Autumn 2025
Format: 3 hours of lab per week
Description: The lab was divided into the following parts:
  • Verilog: Part of digital logic, covers combinational and sequential circuit implementation
  • X86-64 Assembly: Registers, stack, functions, calling conventions as well as structs
  • Performance: Using performance tools such as perf and vtune to recognise microarchitectural bottlenecks
  • Simulator: Extended the Champsim Simulator to model various cache designs
All course material
IIT Bombay | CS236: Operating Systems Lab | Instructor: Mythili Vutukuru
Semester: Spring 2025
Format: 3 hours of lab per week
Description: The course was divided into four parts - processes, memory, concurrency and IO/file systems. For the first 3 parts, students had to implement basic OS functionality by extending the XV6 OS.
